⚙️ Grown-Up Corner

Benji's targets from his school report: /r/ (all positions) and /s/, /z/, plus following directions & longer sentences. This is home practice to support his school speech therapy.

🔊 Talking voice (the model Benji copies)

A crazy-clear kid voice reads the words out loud (free, streams over the internet). If it's ever offline, it automatically falls back to the device voice below.

Fallback / device voice

Used only if the kid voice can't load. Pick the clearest one on this device (look for Enhanced, Natural, or Google):

Best sound in ~20 seconds: iPhone/iPad → Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → download an Enhanced voice. Android → update Google Text-to-speech. Then pick it above.

Mouth cues

R: tongue back & up, growl like a bear — rrr.
S: smile, teeth close, tongue behind top teeth, quiet snake — sss.
Z: like S but voice ON — buzzy bee, zzz.

🎤 How the game checks his words

Pick how strict the game is. All three still let him keep going so he never gets stuck or upset — the difference is how much it asks him to say the word clearly.

Coach uses the device's speech recognition: if it doesn't hear the word clearly, it gently says it again and gives him another try (then moves on kindly). Clear words earn dinosaurs faster. Works best on Chromebook / Android / Chrome. On iPad/iPhone speech recognition is unreliable — there, Grown-up checks is the strict option that always works.

Sound & pacing

The voice is slowed everywhere so Benji hears every sound clearly (the pitch stays natural). Extra slow stretches it even more for the hardest words.

When Benji mis-says a word (his SLP's steps)

Least → most help. Stay warm and playful:

  1. Ask him to clarify — "What did you say?"
  2. Ask it back gently — "The tar?"
  3. Repeat his word as a question — "The tar?"
  4. Give a choice, then model it — "Tar or car? … Say car."

Don't count sound errors against him in reading/writing. Praise good tries — effort first.
